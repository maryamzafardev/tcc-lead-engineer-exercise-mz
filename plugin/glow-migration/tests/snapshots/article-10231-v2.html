As the weather warms up, your skin's needs change. Here's how to reset your routine in about ten minutes.

Step 1: Lighten your cleanser

Swap cream cleansers for a gentle gel. If you're not sure where to start, our drugstore sunscreen guide lists a few budget-friendly picks from the same brands.

Step 2: Edit your actives

  • Keep your vitamin C serum in the morning
  • Use retinol two or three nights a week
  • Never skip SPF

Step 3: Rethink moisture

A lightweight lotion is usually enough. Read more in our moisturiser explainer.
